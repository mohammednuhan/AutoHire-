# AutoHire Demo Video Script

## Scene 1: Upload Resume (0:00-0:30)

Show onboarding. Drag and drop a resume, click Parse Resume, show progress states, then show the editable profile.

## Scene 2: Jobs Are Found (0:30-1:00)

Open Jobs. Show job cards with score badges. Click one card and show score breakdown, matching skills, missing skills, and recommendation.

## Scene 3: Agent Fills a Form (1:00-1:45)

Open Dashboard. Show live feed events: navigating, filling first name, filling email, uploading resume, validation confidence, ready to submit.

## Scene 4: Review and Submit (1:45-2:00)

Open the review screen. Show cover letter, resume preview, and form answers. Click Submit Application and confirm.

## Scene 5: Telegram Notification (2:00-2:10)

Show phone screen with Telegram notification: "Applied to Backend Engineer at Razorpay".

## End Frame
